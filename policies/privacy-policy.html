Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2025

Your Privacy Matters: RhythmFlow is committed to protecting your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. All music processing happens entirely on your device.

Introduction

Welcome to RhythmFlow, an AI-powered music player application developed by TechNexa Innovations. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to your privacy and data protection. We believe in complete transparency about how our app operates.

Information We Do NOT Collect

RhythmFlow is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Music library contents or metadata
  • Listening habits, preferences, or behavior
  • Playlists or user-created content
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports
  • Any other personally identifiable information

How Your Data is Stored

All data generated by RhythmFlow is stored exclusively on your device:

Music Library Data

When you use RhythmFlow, the app scans your device for music files and extracts metadata (song title, artist, album, etc.) from those files. This information is stored locally in a secure database on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

AI Analysis Results

RhythmFlow uses on-device artificial intelligence to analyze your music (genre classification, mood detection, tempo analysis). All AI processing occurs locally on your device using the YAMNet model. Analysis results are stored only on your device.

User Preferences and Playlists

Custom playlists, app settings, listening statistics, and user preferences are stored locally using secure storage mechanisms. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions We Request

To provide its core functionality, RhythmFlow requires certain permissions. Here's what we need and why:

Storage Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO)

  • Purpose: To access and read your local music files
  • Usage: Reading audio files, extracting metadata, and displaying album artwork
  • Privacy Protection: We only read music files; no other files are accessed. Information never leaves your device.

Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Purpose: To display playback controls in the notification panel
  • Usage: Showing current song information and media controls (play, pause, skip)
  • Privacy Protection: Notifications are generated locally and contain no tracking or external communication

Foreground Service & Wake Lock

  • Purpose: To enable background music playback
  • Usage: Keeping music playing when the app is minimized or the screen is locked
  • Privacy Protection: No data is collected or transmitted during background operation

Internet Access

  • Purpose: To check for app updates through the Google Play Store
  • Usage: Silent background update checks using Google Play's in-app update feature
  • Privacy Protection: Communication occurs only with Google Play Store servers for update purposes. No user data is transmitted.

Third-Party Services

RhythmFlow operates with minimal third-party dependencies:

Google Play Store

The app uses Google Play Store services for distribution and automatic updates. When checking for updates, your device communicates with Google's servers. This communication is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours. We do not receive any data from these interactions.

No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use any analytics services, including:

  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Crash reporting services
  • User behavior tracking
  • Advertising networks or SDKs

No Cloud Services

RhythmFlow does not use any cloud storage, cloud sync, or remote backup services. All your data remains exclusively on your device.

Artificial Intelligence Processing

RhythmFlow includes AI-powered features for music analysis:

YAMNet Model

We use Google's open-source YAMNet audio classification model, which runs entirely on your device. This model helps identify genres, detect moods, recognize instruments, and analyze musical characteristics.

On-Device Processing

All AI analysis occurs locally on your device. Your music files are never uploaded to external servers for processing. The AI model works completely offline.

Accuracy Disclaimer

AI-powered features may have varying accuracy due to model limitations. Results should be considered approximate rather than definitive. We provide these features as a best-effort service using available technology.

Data Security

We protect your data through several measures:

  • Local Storage Only: All data is stored securely on your device using encrypted local databases
  • No Network Transmission: Music data, analysis results, and personal preferences are never transmitted over the network
  • App Sandbox: Data is isolated within the app's secure sandbox environment, preventing access by other apps
  • Complete Data Removal: Uninstalling the app removes all associated data from your device

Children's Privacy

RhythmFlow does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we don't collect any personal data, the app is safe for all ages. No account creation or personal information is required to use RhythmFlow.

Your Rights and Control

Since RhythmFlow doesn't collect data, you maintain complete control:

  • Data Ownership: All data belongs to you and remains on your device
  • Data Access: You can view all app data through the app's settings and database
  • Data Deletion: Clear app data through your device settings or uninstall the app to remove all information
  • No Tracking: We cannot and do not track your usage or behavior
  • Offline Functionality: The app works completely offline (except for optional update checks)

International Users

RhythmFlow can be used worldwide. Regardless of your location, all data processing occurs locally on your device. No data crosses international borders because no data is transmitted.

Legal Compliance

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Since we don't collect data, there's no data to share, sell, or process under these regulations.

Transparency and Trust

  • We have no interest in collecting, analyzing, or monetizing your information
  • Our business model does not rely on user data
  • You can verify our privacy practices by reviewing the permissions we request
